Appendix D: Test-Day Checklist

Tick each line as you go. The rules below were checked against the exam owner's candidate ID policy and online testing guidelines on 20 November 2026. Rules change, so read the official candidate pages again in the week before your exam, and follow your appointment confirmation if it says something different.

Key Point: most exams that end badly on the day end for reasons that have nothing to do with security knowledge: the wrong ID, a name that does not match, a late start, or a broken online testing rule. Every line below removes one of those risks.

During the exam

- Look at each PBQ briefly. If it looks long, flag it and move on.

- Answer every multiple choice question in a first pass, and flag the ones you are unsure of.

- Go back to the flagged PBQs. Leave at least 15 minutes for them.

- Use any time left on your flagged multiple choice questions.

After the exam

Remember It: ID, Time, Room, Rules. Two matching IDs, early arrival or check-in, a clean room or a cleared pocket, and no breaks, no notes and no talking.
